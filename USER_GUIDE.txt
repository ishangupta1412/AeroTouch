HOW TO CONTROL YOUR LAPTOP WITH HAND GESTURES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MOVE CURSOR:
   - Bring your RIGHT HAND in front of the webcam.
   - Point your INDEX FINGER and move your hand.
   - Your laptop mouse cursor will move smoothly in real time.

2. LEFT CLICK:
   - Pinch your INDEX FINGER and THUMB together quickly.
   - Your laptop will register a standard Left Click.

3. RIGHT CLICK:
   - Pinch your MIDDLE FINGER and THUMB together.
   - Your laptop will open the Right Click context menu.

4. SNAP ACTIVE WINDOW TO LEFT HALF:
   - Close your LEFT HAND into a FIST (✊).
   - While holding the fist, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - Windows will snap the active window to the left side (Win + Left Arrow).

5. SWITCH TO NEXT VIRTUAL DESKTOP:
   - Raise 3 FINGERS on your LEFT HAND.
   - While holding 3 fingers, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - Windows will flip to the next virtual desktop (Win + Ctrl + Right Arrow).

6. HOW TO EXIT / STOP:
   - Press the 'q' key on your keyboard, or press Ctrl + C in the terminal.
