1. GLOBAL KEYBOARD SHORTCUT (FASTEST):
   Press: Ctrl + Alt + A from anywhere in Windows!
   AeroTouch will immediately launch.

2. 1-CLICK LAUNCHER:
   Double-click "AeroTouch" (or "Start_AeroTouch.bat") on your Desktop!

3. TERMINAL LAUNCH:
   cd "C:\Users\Ishan Gupta\OneDrive\Desktop\AeroTouch"
   python -m src.main
   (Or with ambient floating orb: python -m src.main --orb)

A camera window titled "AeroTouch Spatial Kernel" will open up.

HOW TO CONTROL YOUR LAPTOP WITH GESTURES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MOUSE CURSOR MOVEMENT (ROCK-SOLID STILLNESS):
   - Bring your RIGHT HAND in front of the webcam.
   - Point with your INDEX FINGER and move through the air.
   - Dynamic Spatial Deadband Noise Gate suppresses hand tremors < 4.8px.
   - When you hold your finger still, the cursor locks rock-solid with zero vibration.

2. INTENTIONAL LEFT CLICK (ZERO GHOST CLICKS):
   - Deliberately pinch your THUMB and INDEX FINGER tips together.
   - Multi-Frame Confirmation (2 consecutive frames) guarantees no accidental clicks.
   - Once released, 200ms hysteresis prevents double-click flutter.

3. RIGHT CLICK (CONTEXT MENU):
   - Pinch your THUMB and MIDDLE FINGER tips together.
   - Context menu opens cleanly without moving the pointer.

4. SNAP ACTIVE WINDOW TO LEFT HALF:
   - Make a FIST with your LEFT HAND.
   - While holding the fist, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - The active window snaps to the left half of your screen.

5. SWITCH TO NEXT VIRTUAL DESKTOP:
   - Raise 3 FINGERS on your LEFT HAND.
   - While holding 3 fingers, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - Windows switches to your next virtual desktop.

6. HOW TO EXIT / STOP:
   - Click the camera window and press the 'q' key.

AEROTOUCH CORTEX: AUTONOMOUS BACKGROUND KINEMATIC COACH:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AeroTouch runs a local AI coach powered by your Ollama 'aerotouch-qwen:latest'
model (built on Qwen 2.5).

How it works:
1. While you use AeroTouch, your session telemetry (pinch distance distribution,
   finger velocity, tremor amplitude) is recorded to data/sessions/.
2. When you exit with 'q', Qwen analyzes your session data in the background.
3. Qwen calculates personalized parameter adjustments tailored to your exact hand
   geometry and writes them to data/user_profile.json.
4. Next time you launch AeroTouch, it automatically boots with your fine-tuned
   profile for maximum precision!
