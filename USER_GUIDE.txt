HOW TO CONTROL YOUR LAPTOP WITH GESTURES (ZERO-LAG 60 FPS):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MOUSE CURSOR MOVEMENT:
   - Bring your RIGHT HAND in front of the webcam.
   - Point with your INDEX FINGER and move through the air.
   - Cursor moves smoothly in real-time across your display.

2. LEFT CLICK (Normal Select / Open):
   - Pinch your THUMB and INDEX FINGER tips together.
   - Your laptop will register an atomic left click immediately!

3. RIGHT CLICK (Context Menu):
   - Pinch your THUMB and MIDDLE FINGER tips together.
   - Context menu opens instantly!

4. SNAP ACTIVE WINDOW TO LEFT HALF:
   - Make a FIST (✊) with your LEFT HAND.
   - While holding the fist, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - The active window snaps to the left half of your screen.

5. SWITCH TO NEXT VIRTUAL DESKTOP:
   - Raise 3 FINGERS on your LEFT HAND.
   - While holding 3 fingers, PINCH with your RIGHT HAND.
   - Windows switches to your next virtual desktop.

6. HOW TO EXIT / STOP:
   - Click the camera window and press the 'q' key.
