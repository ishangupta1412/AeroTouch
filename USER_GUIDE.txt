HOW TO START (SUPER EASY - 1 CLICK):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply double-click "Start_AeroTouch.bat" on your Desktop!
Or open terminal and run:
   cd "C:\Users\Ishan Gupta\Desktop\AeroTouch"
   python -m src.main

A camera window titled "AeroTouch Spatial Kernel" will open up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO CONTROL YOUR LAPTOP WITH GESTURES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MOUSE CURSOR MOVEMENT:
   - Apne RIGHT HAND ko webcam ke aage laayein.
   - Index finger point karke hawa me move karein.
   - Screen par cursor smoothly move karega.

2. LEFT CLICK (Normal Click / Select / Open):
   - Thumb aur Index Finger ke tips ko aapas me touch/pinch karein (👌 pinch gesture).
   - Camera screen par green line banegi aur upar flash hoga:
     >> LEFT CLICK <<
   - Sensitivity ko 0.38 tak relax kar diya hai, toh halka pinch karte hi click ho jayega!

3. RIGHT CLICK (Context Menu / Options):
   - Thumb aur Middle Finger (beech wali ungli) ke tips ko touch/pinch karein.
   - Camera screen par orange line banegi aur upar flash hoga:
     >> RIGHT CLICK <<

4. SNAP WINDOW TO LEFT (Multitasking):
   - Apne LEFT HAND ki mutthi (fist ✊) banayein.
   - Mutthi bane rehne par RIGHT HAND se pinch karein.
   - Jo window khuli hai wo screen ke left half par snap ho jayegi (Win + Left).

5. SWITCH DESKTOP (Next Virtual Desktop):
   - Apne LEFT HAND ki 3 ungliyan uthayein (3 fingers up).
   - RIGHT HAND se pinch karein.
   - Windows next virtual desktop pe switch ho jayega (Ctrl + Win + Right).

6. HOW TO STOP / EXIT:
   - Camera window pe click karke keyboard se 'q' press karein.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LIVE ON-SCREEN HUD (VISUAL FEEDBACK):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera window me aapki ungliyon ke upar live numeric distance dikhta hai:
  "Pinch: 0.22 (Limit: 0.38)"
- Jab bhi aapka pinch limit ke andar aata hai, screen par instant notification
  flash hoti hai taaki aapko pata chale ki click register ho gaya!
